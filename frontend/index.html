Plant bio-electric monitoring

Plants speak in millivolts.
PhytoSense listens.

A surface bio-potential sensor, a real-time DSP pipeline, and a trained classifier turn a plant's raw electrical signal into a readable state — then an AI assistant explains what it means and answers questions about it, in plain language.

How it works

  1. 1

    Sense

    An electrode pair captures the plant's surface bio-potential — a slow-moving electrical signal that shifts with hydration, light, and touch.

  2. 2

    Filter

    A 50 Hz notch filter strips mains interference; a moving average smooths sensor noise, leaving the genuine physiological trend.

  3. 3

    Classify

    Six extracted features feed a Random Forest model that scores the signal against Healthy, Mild Stress, and Water Deficit.

  4. 4

    Explain

    An LLM turns the classification and readouts into a plain status update, and stays available to answer follow-up questions.
